# Delete Your Account

Alternun provides a dedicated, accessible account-deletion page in the AIRS app. It includes a language selector and clear sign-in and settings actions.

## Start a deletion request

1. Open [airs.alternun.co/delete-account](https://airs.alternun.co/delete-account).
2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
3. Review the information you need to save, then contact support from the account's registered email address so the request can be verified and completed safely.

If you cannot sign in, contact [support@alternun.co](mailto:support@alternun.co) and include the email address associated with the account.
